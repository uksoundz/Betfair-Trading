Free. Gives you today's and future fixtures for the Premier League, Championship, Bundesliga, La Liga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga and Champions League, plus fresh results for the ratings.

Betfair prices

Optional but important: with Betfair connected the app compares its own numbers with the real exchange price and shows the Edge. That is where profit comes from.

  1. You need a funded Betfair account.
  2. Follow developer.betfair.com/get-started to create an Application Key. The free Delayed key is fine to start.
  3. Enter the key plus your Betfair username and password. They are stored only on this computer, in the .env file next to the app.

Staking

Stake suggestions are a fraction of the mathematically optimal (Kelly) stake, capped at 5% of bank. Smaller is safer.

Betting

Controls what the Bet slip is allowed to do. Off: review and copy only. Paper: record slips in My picks without placing anything. Live: after you press a confirmation button, the app sends the slip's lines to Betfair as limit orders at the plan price. Unmatched orders lapse at kick-off. Nothing is ever sent without that confirmation.

How to use TradeScout

  1. Set up once in Settings: paste your free football-data.org key for live fixtures. Add Betfair when you are ready to see real prices and edge.
  2. Each day: open the app, it shows the next day with matches. Top picks is the ranked shortlist for that day. Click a pick to open the full plan.
  3. Matches lists every fixture. Click one for the model's view in plain English and every strategy that fits.
  4. By strategy answers "which matches suit lay the draw today?" and tells you when a strategy should and should not be used.
  5. Track the picks you like. My picks settles them automatically against the real results and keeps your strike rate, profit and return. Paper trade for a few weeks before staking real money.

Reading a plan

Each plan has Before kick-off (what to place and the price), In play (what to wait for), Get out (how to take profit), Stop loss (when to cut) and Why, the reasoning behind the numbers. The scenario table shows every way the match can go, how likely each is and what you make or lose.

Ratings

  • ★★★★★ / ★★★★ strong: the expected return after costs is clearly positive.
  • ★★★ good at the price shown or better. ★★ fair, marginal. ★ leave it.
  • Pays off is how often this plan ends in profit, adjusted by how the strategy has really performed in this league over two seasons of back-testing.
  • Edge is how much better the exchange price is than the model's fair price. It only appears with Betfair connected. Without it, ratings are driven by the model alone and cluster around ★★ to ★★★.
  • Confidence is how much recent data sits behind both teams. Treat anything under 0.5 with care (new or promoted teams, early season).

Rules that keep you profitable

  • Only enter at the plan's price or better. If the market does not offer it, there is no trade.
  • Decide the exit and the stop before kick-off. The plan gives you both. Follow them.
  • Stakes are small on purpose. Variance is the thing that ends trading careers.
  • Prefer liquid leagues (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Champions League).
  • This is research, not tips. The in-play decisions are yours.
