Betfair

Needed for anything to be called a TRADE: the app compares its probabilities with the exchange's back and lay prices and depth. Without it every idea is RESEARCH ONLY.

  1. Funded Betfair account.
  2. Create an Application Key at developer.betfair.com/get-started (the free Delayed key is fine to start).
  3. Enter the key, username and password. Stored only in the .env file on this computer.
Two-factor authentication on the account? Certificate login

Betfair accounts with two-factor authentication cannot log in with a password alone. Create a client certificate as described at Betfair: non-interactive login, upload it to your Betfair account, and give the two file paths here.

How to read TradeScout

  1. Pick a sport at the top: Football or Tennis. Each has its own fixtures, model and strategies.
  2. Pick a day. Today, any future day, or a past day to see what happened.
  3. Opportunities ranks ideas. Each carries a decision: TRADE conservative net edge after commission clears your threshold at a price that is actually available; NO TRADE priced by the exchange but no proven advantage; RESEARCH no exchange price, so nothing can be called value. Most days have few or no TRADEs. That is the system working.
  4. Open a plan for the market, side, entry price, maximum price, stake, maximum loss, why the model sees value, what must happen, when to exit, and what to do if it goes wrong.
  5. Place on Betfair from any plan in the Matches view (Betfair connected, Betting set to Live): the slip shows every line with the live price, you press Place, then confirm. A TRADE places directly; anything else needs the override tick-box on the slip. Placing is always your decision, one slip at a time.
  6. Exchange prices refresh themselves every minute while Betfair is connected (the countdown is next to the date). The pill at the top right says how many of the day's fixtures are priced; click it for the feed details: which fixtures the exchange could not be matched to and why, in-play or suspended matches, session and key status, and a full diagnosis.
  7. Track picks into My picks to build a record. Performance shows out-of-sample strategy statistics and what the signals log has recorded.

The numbers on a card

  • Pays off: probability the plan ends in profit, shrunk towards the strategy's real out-of-sample strike rate.
  • Net edge: expected profit per £1 risked after commission, using a probability pulled towards the market's own price. The model is only allowed a small say against the exchange.
  • Confidence: how much data sits behind the ratings. Low confidence pulls the probability further towards the market.
  • Execution: can the size be matched at the plan price, and is the spread tight.
  • Evidence: how the plan's return is established. "Exchange-priced, settles at result" is firmest. "Simulated in-play exits" means the exits are the model's estimate of where the market will be, not recorded prices.
  • Score: 10 points per 1% conservative net edge, scaled by execution and evidence. Research ideas never score above 39.

What is and is not proven

The out-of-sample tests show the football match-odds model has modest skill over league averages and the tennis ratings barely beat the ATP ranking, while exchange prices are known to be sharp. No historical exchange prices were available to prove that any strategy made money against the market. Until the signals log shows TRADE decisions paying off at observed prices, treat every return figure as an estimate, not a track record.
