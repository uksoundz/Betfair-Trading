How to use TradeScout

  1. Pick a day at the top and press Scan this day. Any day works: today, this weekend, or a past Saturday to see how the picks did.
  2. Matches lists every fixture with its best score. Click one for the full breakdown: the model's view of the match, then every strategy that fits with a step-by-step plan.
  3. Top picks is the ranked shortlist across the whole day. Start here each morning.
  4. By strategy answers "which matches suit lay-the-draw today?" and tells you when a strategy should and should not be used.

Reading a plan

Each plan is split into Before kick-off (what to place and at what price), In play (what to wait for), Get out (how to take profit) and Stop loss (when to cut and run). The "Why" section is the model's reasoning in plain words. The scenario table shows every way the match can go, how likely it is, and what you make or lose in each case.

The numbers

  • Score 0-100. 50 is break-even after costs. Green at 60+, amber 50-60, grey below.
  • Pays off the probability the plan ends in profit, adjusted by how this strategy has really performed in this league.
  • Return expected profit per £1 risked. Stake is a deliberately small suggestion based on your bank.
  • Edge how much better the exchange price is than the model's fair price. Only shown when Betfair is connected. This is where profit comes from, so connect Betfair as soon as you are comfortable.
  • Confidence how much recent data sits behind both teams. Treat anything under 0.5 with care.

Rules

  • Paper trade first. Scan a few past Saturdays and check the results against the plans.
  • Decide your exit and your stop before kick-off. The plan gives you both.
  • Prices in the plans are what the model expects. Real markets will differ, especially in smaller leagues. Only enter at the price shown or better.
  • This is research, not tips. The in-play decisions are yours.
